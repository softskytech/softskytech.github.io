This Privacy Policy explains what information ScaleSnap may collect, how it may be used, and how it may be shared when you use our websites, mobile sites, applications, and related services.

ScaleSnap strongly believes in keeping your personal information confidential. The following policy describes what information we collect and how it is used and shared.

Information We Collect

When you register with us or use ScaleSnap, you generally provide your measurement preferences and device camera access for the purpose of providing our image analysis and measurement services. Camera and photo library permissions are requested only when needed for app features, such as taking a photo or selecting an image to analyze.

In addition, ScaleSnap may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your mobile device's unique device identifiers where available and permitted, the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, the type of mobile Internet browsers you use, and information about the way you use ScaleSnap.

In general, this means that we may collect the following types of information:

  • Device information and identifiers.
  • Camera and photo library access, with your permission.
  • Photos you choose to capture or select for analysis.
  • Measurement preferences, including metric or imperial units.
  • Selected scanner type, such as weight, measurements, calories, plants, translation, or object counting.
  • Usage data, app events, diagnostics, and analytics.
  • Purchase, subscription, and restore-purchase status where applicable.
  • Support messages and contact information if you contact us.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate and improve ScaleSnap, provide app features, process photos you choose to analyze, remember your measurement preferences, display results, maintain scan history, provide customer support, troubleshoot errors, protect against misuse, and manage purchases or subscriptions.

We may work with analytics companies to help us understand how ScaleSnap is being used, such as the frequency and duration of usage. We may also work with advertisers or third-party advertising networks, who may need to know how you interact with advertising provided in ScaleSnap. This helps us make the ScaleSnap experience better.

We may also share depersonalized or aggregated versions of information from your application instances in order to enable product analysis, performance analysis, fraud prevention, advertising-related use, or service improvement.

Information Sharing

You agree that in order to provide services to you which you have requested, including relevant content, measurement results, app functionality, purchases, support, analytics, and advertising, we may exchange information that we collect from you with third parties who help us perform these tasks and provide these products and services.

This information may include device information, app identifiers, advertising identifiers where available and permitted by platform settings, vendor identifiers, device system information, application software information, peripheral information, country or region settings on the device, camera and photo library access status, measurement preferences, usage data, and analytics information.

We do not require you to provide more information than is reasonably necessary to use ScaleSnap. However, some features may not function if camera, photo library, network, or purchase-related permissions are unavailable.

Photos And Camera Access

ScaleSnap uses your camera and photo library only with your permission. You may use the camera to capture a photo or choose an existing image from your library. These images are used to provide the analysis or measurement feature you request, such as estimating weight, dimensions, calories, plant identity, translation, or object count.

You can manage camera and photo permissions in your device settings. If permissions are disabled, camera capture or gallery selection may not be available.

Measurement Results And Scan History

ScaleSnap may store scan results and related images on your device so you can review previous results in scan history. You can remove app data by deleting the app or using available in-app controls where provided.

ScaleSnap results are estimates generated from photos and should not be treated as certified measurements, medical nutrition advice, legal advice, safety-critical information, or professional advice.
